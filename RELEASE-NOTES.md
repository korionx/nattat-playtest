# Nattat 0.5.0 — Clash Update

- Aimed shoulder strikes with bounded impact strength.
- Aerial pounces that rebound the attacker upward.
- Symmetric clashes and fresh-burst counters.
- Contact-point streaks and rings, recoil animation, original layered impact sounds and burst whooshes.
- Air control, recovery opportunities and gentle-effects support remain available.

Install 0.5.0 on every device for multiplayer. Windows and Android can play together on the same reachable local network. Public Internet matchmaking is not deployed. These are early direct-download playtest builds, not store releases.

The included trailers use actual rendered gameplay with staged starting positions and scripted inputs. Music and sound design are original. Landscape and vertical trailers are 30 seconds; the short vertical cut is 15 seconds.

Tested: 69 simulation checks, Windows/Android/Linux builds, party-service tests, and four-client local networking with simulated latency/input loss. Physical-phone combat feel, performance and speaker balance still need tester feedback.

Jumper: run before jumping, aim your burst into a rival to strike, burst down from above to pounce, or meet an attack with a fresh burst to counter. Landing restores your burst. Builders can place recovery springs; every spring is public.
